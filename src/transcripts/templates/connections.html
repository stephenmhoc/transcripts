Bring your history

In Overcast’s account page, choose Export Your Data → All data. The plain subscription OPML does not contain episode activity.

A gentle first sync.

The initial import queues qualifying activity from the last 7 days, up to 20 episodes per check. Older entries and unknown dates stay in history for manual selection. Partial episodes qualify after 5 minutes of progress.

A read-only connection to your whole library: podcasts, episodes, listening observations, timestamped transcripts, search, and pipeline health.

Use Streamable HTTP with Authorization: Bearer <MCP_TOKEN>. The separate reader key is in your private server configuration.

Muse must support a bearer header and have a network path into your tailnet. A hosted client may need an approved gateway or OAuth; connecting the actual client is a separate setup step.
